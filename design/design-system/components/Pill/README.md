# Pill

A rounded navy label with gold text that introduces a block: «خدماتنا» and «العنوان» on the roll-up banner, the eyebrow above every section title on the website.

- **Provide** one to three words. Markup: `<span class="ra-pill">`; add `ra-pill--eyebrow` above section titles (12px uppercase with 0.06em tracking in English; plain `label` in Arabic, Urdu and Bangla).
- Solid: `navy-slab` ground, `accent-on-navy` text (8:1). Inside a `.ra-navy` band it lifts to navy-600. `ra-pill--outline`: transparent with a gold `rule` outline, for photos and busy grounds.
- Corner `radius-pill`. Align it with the title it introduces (start-aligned, or centred in `.ra-sechead--center`).
- **Don't** use it as a button, a filter tab or a status badge.
