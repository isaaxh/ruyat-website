# Footer

The navy footer: logo and a short line about Ruyat, quick links, services, contact rows, social buttons and a bottom line.

- **Markup:** `footer.ra-footer.ra-navy` › `.ra-wrap` › `.ra-footer__grid` with four columns: brand (`img.ra-footer__logo` = `ruyat-lockup-on-dark.png`, `p.ra-footer__about`, `.ra-social`), Quick Links, Services, Contact (each `h2.ra-footer__title` + `ul.ra-footer__links` of `a.ra-footer__row`) › `.ra-footer__bottom` (© year Ruyat Al Injaz · `[DESIGN CREDIT]`).
- 1 column on mobile, 2 on tablet, 1.6 : 1 : 1 : 1 on desktop. Column titles: English 12px uppercase gold with 0.08em tracking; other languages `label` size, no caps.
- Social, in this order: WhatsApp · Instagram · TikTok · X · Snapchat · YouTube: 44px gold-outlined circles with the platform icon and an `aria-label`.
- Rows `size-tap` high, `on-navy-muted`, white on hover; contact rows carry an 18px gold icon.
