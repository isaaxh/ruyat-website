# Partners

Logos of partner companies, as supplied by each partner. Each comes as SVG (use on the web), PNG (transparent, 2000px wide; documents and social) and PDF (print). Shown in the PartnerNetwork section.

**Al Faisal Group and Partners** (مجموعة الفيصل وشركاؤه), `al-faisal/`
- `alfaisal-logo-ar.*`: full logo with Arabic name and line. Default on Arabic pages and in PartnerNetwork.
- `alfaisal-logo-en.*`: full logo with English name. Default on English pages.
- `alfaisal-fl-monogram.svg` / `alfaisal-fl.png`: the FL monogram alone, where space is tight.

**Ruyat Shams** (رؤية شمس), fruits, vegetables and catering, `ruyat-shams/`
- `ruyat-shams-1-full.*`: icon, bilingual name and tagline. Default in PartnerNetwork.
- `ruyat-shams-2-name.*`: icon and name, no tagline.
- `ruyat-shams-3-tagline-horizontal.*`: horizontal lockup for wide, short spaces.
- `ruyat-shams-4-icon.*`: the eye-and-sun icon alone.

Place them on white or light grounds, contained and centred; never recolour, crop or redraw them.
